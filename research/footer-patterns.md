# Footer pattern check

Checked 2026-08-15 for the Team 812 footer cleanup.

## Pattern

- OpenAI uses a full-width footer with grouped navigation, social links, policy links, and locale controls.
- Apple uses a full-width utility footer with grouped product, support, legal, and region links rather than a boxed identity card.
- Team 254 and Citrus Circuits use compact team-site footers centered on navigation, contact, and social links.

## Team 812 decision

Use one continuous footer background, remove the inset logo treatment, remove the eyebrow, and keep the team name, contact address, and social links. Preserve the existing chant as the team's small personality detail.
